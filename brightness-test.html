How to Test Screen Brightness

Use the browser patterns and on-screen display together.

STEP 01

Set a stable environment

Keep room lighting steady and turn off automatic brightness, local dimming or dynamic contrast when practical.

STEP 02

Compare gray and white

View the same gray or white field while changing the display's brightness control. Look for comfortable visibility and consistent appearance.

STEP 03

Check dark appearance

Use black and dark gray to inspect shadow visibility in your room. Viewing angle and panel technology can affect what you see.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this brightness test measure nits?

No. It displays patterns but cannot measure physical screen luminance. A calibrated luminance meter is needed for a nit reading.

Why does brightness appear different in fullscreen?

Ambient light, HDR, automatic brightness, local dimming and display picture modes can all change apparent output.

Can I use it on a phone or TV?

Yes. It runs in a modern browser on phones, tablets, laptops, monitors and TVs.
