How to Test Your Camera

Camera access remains off until you choose to start.

STEP 01

Press Start Camera

Choose Start Camera, then allow access in the browser permission prompt. If the prompt was previously blocked, update this site's camera permission in browser settings.

STEP 02

Check preview and settings

Confirm the preview appears, choose another listed camera if needed, and review the resolution and frame rate currently reported for the video track.

STEP 03

Stop when finished

Use Stop Camera to end the stream and release the camera. Download a snapshot only if you want a local still image saved to your device.

i

Camera Access & Measurement Notes

Webcam access and reported settings are controlled by the browser and operating system.

Secure context and permission

Camera access is generally available on HTTPS websites or localhost. The browser displays its own permission prompt; this page cannot bypass it. A camera may also be unavailable while another application is using it.

Settings can vary

The browser may negotiate a resolution and frame rate based on camera capabilities, lighting, bandwidth or system load. Reported settings are for the current stream, not a lab measurement or guarantee of device maximums.

Privacy by design

The page requests camera access only after Start Camera is pressed. Video stays in the local browser preview, tracks are stopped when you use Stop Camera, and snapshots are downloaded locally only on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this camera test upload video?

No. The preview runs in your browser. The optional snapshot is created locally and downloaded only when you choose.

When does the page ask for permission?

Only after you press Start Camera. Stop Camera ends the active stream and releases its tracks.

Why does camera access require HTTPS?

Modern browsers generally allow camera access only in secure contexts such as HTTPS or localhost.

Are resolution and frame rate guaranteed specifications?

No. They are settings reported by the current browser video track and may vary from the camera's advertised maximum.

Can I choose another camera?

After permission is granted, available video inputs appear in the selector. Choosing another camera switches the active stream.

Why is the camera list empty before starting?

Browsers may hide camera labels and device details until camera permission is granted. Start the test to request permission and refresh the list.
