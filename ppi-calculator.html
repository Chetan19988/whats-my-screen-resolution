What Is PPI (Pixels Per Inch)?

PPI is pixels per linear inch. It shows how tightly pixels are packed. Higher PPI means sharper text and less visible pixel structure at the same viewing distance.

Example: 27-inch 4K is about 163 PPI. A 6.1-inch phone at 1170x2532 is about 457 PPI. The phone has fewer total pixels but higher density because it is much smaller.

How to Calculate PPI

Divide diagonal pixels by diagonal inches.

PPI = sqrt(w*w + h*h) / diagonal_inches

PPI Calculation Example

1920x1080 at 24in: sqrt(1920*1920+1080*1080)=2202.9. 2202.9/24=91.8 PPI.

How to Calculate Pixels Per Square Inch (PPI2)

PPI is linear. PPI squared estimates pixels per square inch for square pixels.

PPI2 Formula

PPI2 = PPI x PPI

PPI2 Calculation Example

109 PPI: 109x109=11881 pixels per sq inch.

How to Calculate Screen Pixel Density

Pixel density links resolution, size, and area. Use PPI for linear sharpness. Use total pixels divided by area for areal density. Both rise when resolution rises or size shrinks.

PPI vs Resolution: Whats the Difference?

Resolution is total pixels. PPI is density. Same resolution on larger screen means lower PPI.

What Does Higher PPI Mean?

More detail per inch, smoother text edges, less visible pixel grid.

Does Higher Resolution Always Mean Higher PPI?

No. 1080p on 24in is 92 PPI. 1080p on 6in phone is higher PPI. Size matters.

How Screen Size Affects PPI

For fixed resolution, PPI falls as diagonal grows. For fixed size, PPI rises with resolution.

PPI vs DPI: Whats the Difference?

PPI is display pixels per inch. DPI is printer dots per inch. They are not the same, though often mixed in casual use.

How to Calculate Aspect Ratio

Divide width and height by their GCD.

Aspect Ratio Formula

AR = w/gcd : h/gcd

Common Display Aspect Ratios

16:9 for most monitors and TVs, 16:10 for some laptops, 21:9 for ultrawide, 4:3 for older panels. Check with our screen resolution checker.

How to Calculate Pixel Pitch

Pixel pitch is mm per pixel, inverse of density.

Pixel Pitch Formula

pitch_mm = 25.4 / PPI

PPI vs Pixel Pitch

Higher PPI means smaller pitch. Example: 92 PPI = 0.276mm. 163 PPI = 0.156mm. 460 PPI = 0.055mm.

PPI and Retina Display Distance

What Is Retina Distance?

Distance where pixels blend for normal vision. Closer screens need higher PPI.

How Viewing Distance Affects Pixel Visibility

Same PPI looks sharper from farther away. Phones need 300+ PPI. Monitors look fine at 90-160 PPI. TVs look fine at 40-80 PPI.

PPI Required for a Retina-Like Display

Rule of thumb: phone 300+, laptop 200+, desktop 100-160 at normal distance. This varies by eyesight.

What Is a Good PPI?

Good PPI depends on distance, not a single best number.

PPI for Monitors

90-110 is standard. 140-165 is sharp for 4K 27-32in.

PPI for Laptops

140-220 is common and looks crisp at arm length.

PPI for Smartphones

350-500 is typical. Above 400 differences are hard to see.

PPI for TVs

40-80 is normal because you sit far away.

Does PPI Affect Image Quality?

PPI and Image Sharpness

Higher PPI gives finer detail if source resolution is high enough.

PPI and Text Clarity

Text benefits most. 100+ PPI desktop and 300+ PPI phone look clean.

PPI and Pixelation

Low PPI shows blocky edges. High PPI hides the grid.

PPI vs Screen Resolution

Resolution is total pixels. PPI is pixels per inch. Use resolution for workload and screen space. Use PPI for sharpness. Verify actual pixels with our screen resolution checker and smoothness with FPS test.

Frequently Asked Questions About PPI

What does PPI mean?

Pixels Per Inch. Pixels packed into one linear inch of display.

How do I calculate PPI?

Use sqrt(w*w+h*h)/diagonal_inches. Enter values above for instant result.

What is a good PPI for a monitor?

90-110 is standard. 140-165 is sharp for 4K. Choice depends on distance and eyesight.

Is higher PPI always better?

Not always. Beyond retina distance you see little gain, but GPU cost rises.

What is the PPI of a 1080p screen?

Depends on size: 92 at 24in, 82 at 27in.

What is the PPI of a 1440p screen?

109 at 27in, 93 at 32in.

What is the PPI of a 4K screen?

163 at 27in, 138 at 32in, 80 at 55in TV.

Is PPI the same as DPI?

No. PPI is screen pixels. DPI is print dots.

Does screen size affect PPI?

Yes. Same resolution on larger screen lowers PPI.

How does PPI affect viewing distance?

Higher PPI lets you sit closer without seeing pixels. Lower PPI needs more distance.
