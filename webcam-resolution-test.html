Frequently Asked Questions

Does the selected size prove my webcam supports it?

No. Compare the actual track dimensions after the browser negotiates the stream. Some requested modes may be unavailable.

When is camera permission requested?

Only after you press Start Test. Stop the test to release the camera track.

Why did I get a smaller resolution?

The camera, browser, operating system or current configuration may not provide the requested size. Try Default or a lower request.
