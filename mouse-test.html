Understanding the Readings

Dashboard numbers reflect events delivered to this page, not certified hardware measurements.

Movement and speed

Coordinates and distance are calculated from pointer events delivered to the test area. Peak speed uses the distance and time between observed movement events. Browser scheduling, acceleration, display scaling and event coalescing can affect these values.

Click rate and double-clicks

Clicks per second is the recent count of click events per one-second window. Double-clicks count the browser's dblclick event; operating-system double-click settings and browser behavior influence recognition.

Not a polling-rate or DPI meter

This page cannot read a mouse's internal polling rate, sensor resolution, DPI/CPI, latency, switches or firmware. Event intervals in a browser are not hardware polling-rate measurements. Use manufacturer utilities or suitable measurement equipment when you need those specifications.

⌕

If a Mouse Input Does Not Register

Try these basic checks before concluding a button or sensor has failed.

Reconnect the mouse

For a wired mouse, reconnect the cable or use a different USB port. For wireless models, check battery charge, receiver placement and Bluetooth connection.

Check device settings

Confirm buttons have not been remapped or disabled in the operating system or vendor software. Test another browser or computer if available.

Clean and inspect

Check the sensor window and scroll wheel for dust or obstruction. Use a suitable clean surface and follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can this mouse test check?

It shows browser-received movement, button presses, click and double-click events, and scroll-wheel events. It cannot diagnose internal mouse hardware or guarantee that each feature is supported.

Can it measure polling rate or DPI?

No. Browser event timing is affected by the browser, operating system and workload; it is not a hardware polling-rate measurement. This page does not measure DPI/CPI.

Why does right-click not open a menu in the pad?

The test area suppresses the context menu so the right button can be tested. Normal browser context menus still work outside the test area.

Does it work with a trackpad?

Yes. A trackpad can test pointer movement, clicks and scrolling. Available buttons and gestures depend on the device and operating-system settings.

Why are the back and forward buttons not detected?

Side buttons are optional, and some mice or browsers do not expose them as standard pointer buttons. Device software may also remap them.

What does the polling-rate panel measure?

It counts pointer movement events delivered to the page during a rolling second. This browser event-rate estimate is not a direct reading of the mouse's hardware polling frequency.

How is the CPS score calculated?

The CPS panel counts click events during a five-second round and divides the total by the round duration for an approximate clicks-per-second score.

Does this mouse test upload data?

No. The test processes interactions locally in your browser and does not send mouse events to a server.
