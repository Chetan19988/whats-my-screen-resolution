How to Test Display Contrast

Use a repeatable setup for a meaningful visual check.

STEP 01

Stabilize the environment

Use consistent room lighting and a comfortable viewing angle. Let the screen warm up before detailed inspection.

STEP 02

Inspect dark and bright steps

Compare near-black and near-white levels. Look for steps that disappear or areas that change relative to neighboring regions.

STEP 03

Adjust carefully

If changing brightness or contrast in the display menu, move one control at a time and revisit the same patterns.

Understanding the Limits

This browser contrast checker supports visual inspection only.

What you can observe

You can visually compare whether the displayed tonal steps are distinguishable under your current browser, display settings, viewing angle and lighting.

Not a contrast-ratio meter

The page cannot read back panel brightness or black level. It does not calculate a hardware contrast ratio or certify display performance. Quantitative testing needs calibrated instruments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a contrast test show?

It provides patterns for visually inspecting dark shadow detail, bright highlight detail and grayscale separation. It does not measure a contrast ratio.

Why use a dark room?

Lower, stable ambient light can make near-black differences easier to see. Avoid changing the room lighting while comparing patterns.

Why are gray steps hard to distinguish?

Display settings, ambient light, viewing angle, panel behavior and browser rendering can all affect how the steps appear.

Can this tool certify my monitor?

No. It is a visual reference. Use suitable calibrated equipment for quantitative contrast measurement.
