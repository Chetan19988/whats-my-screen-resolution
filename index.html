How to Check Screen Resolution by Device

Every device exposes screen resolution differently. Review our clear guide on how to check phone resolution to map out hardware properties manually, or read the structural card tips across multiple platforms below.

Windows 10 & 11

Right-click desktop → Display Settings → Display Resolution. The “Recommended” value is your native resolution. This is the quickest path to figure out my screen resolution or answer what is my pc resolution.

Mac / MacBook Pro

Go to System Settings → Displays. Hover over the highlighted option to see actual pixel values. macOS uses HiDPI/Retina scaling by default, which changes what is my screen resolution mac outputs compared to physical pixels.

Android Phones & Tablets

Go to Settings → Display → Screen Resolution. To dive deeper into system modifications, read our explicit layout resource detailing how to check screen resolution android panels.

iPhone & iPad

iOS does not expose resolution in Settings. Your logical viewport is shown above. Physical pixels are always a DPR multiple (e.g., Retina = 2× or 3×), which dictates the unique phone screen resolution specifications.

Chromebook / Linux

Go to Settings → Device → Displays. The resolution shown is the logical resolution; actual display output may be scaled for your DPI, affecting your current desktop size metrics.

Any Device (Online)

Simply open this page in any browser — your full display metrics are detected instantly with no login or installation required. It is the easiest way to find my screen resolution on the go. Then verify motion smoothness with our online frame rate (FPS) tester.

What is my screen resolution in DPI and inches?

DPI (dots per inch) = physical resolution ÷ screen size in inches. A 1920×1080 panel on a 24″ monitor is approximately 92 DPI. If you ask what are my screen dimensions or what is the screen resolution of my phone in physical inches, you will need to check your device manufacturer spec sheet.

What is Device Pixel Ratio (DPR) and why does it matter?

Device Pixel Ratio (DPR) is the math behind high-density displays. If you want to know how to check my phone resolution or find my phone resolution layout details, DPR is key. A ratio of 2 or 3 means the screen packs multiple physical pixels into a single CSS pixel unit for ultra-sharp rendering.

What aspect ratio is my phone and how is it calculated?

Aspect ratio is the proportional width-to-height relationship of your display. If you are wondering what aspect ratio is my phone or what is the screen ratio of my phone, it is calculated by dividing both resolution dimensions by their greatest common divisor. Modern mobile devices often sport widescreen shapes to accommodate movie playback.
