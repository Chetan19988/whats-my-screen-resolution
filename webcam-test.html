Webcam Test FAQ

Does the webcam test upload video?

No. The video is rendered locally in the browser and is not sent to this site's server.

When is permission requested?

Only when you press Start Webcam. You can stop the stream at any time.

Why is the camera selector empty?

Browsers may hide camera labels until access is granted. Start the test and check that the device is connected.

Why do I need HTTPS?

Modern browsers generally allow webcam access only in a secure context such as HTTPS or localhost.
