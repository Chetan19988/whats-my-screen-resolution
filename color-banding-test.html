What to Look For

  • Visible stripes across a smooth gradient
  • Sudden jumps between nearby tones
  • Posterized regions instead of gradual color change
  • Differences between grayscale and color ramps

Testing Tips

Use fullscreen and a normal viewing distance. Compare patterns with the same browser, picture mode and ambient lighting.

Try a standard or sRGB picture mode and disable extra processing if practical.

Banding may originate from source values, browser rendering, graphics pipeline, display processing or compression. One visible pattern cannot identify its cause.

How to Test for Color Banding

Compare the gradients at a consistent viewing distance and display mode.

STEP 01

Choose a gradient

Begin with grayscale, then inspect individual red, green and blue ramps and the color spectrum.

STEP 02

Look for abrupt transitions

Scan across the gradient for stripes or large tone jumps where you expect gradual change.

STEP 03

Compare settings

If you change color depth or picture settings, revisit the same pattern. Keep in mind browser and source rendering can also contribute.

i

Understanding Color Banding

Visible bands do not necessarily mean the panel itself is defective.

Possible contributors

Banding can be affected by the source image, compression, browser compositing, graphics pipeline, color depth, display mode and panel behavior.

Visual test, not bit-depth analysis

This browser page does not measure panel bit depth, color accuracy or hardware health. Treat the pattern as an inspection aid rather than a certification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is color banding?

Color banding is the appearance of visible stripes or steps in an area that should transition smoothly between colors or shades.

How do I test a monitor for banding?

View fullscreen smooth grayscale and color gradients and look for abrupt stripes or posterized transitions.

Does this measure display color depth?

No. It displays browser-rendered gradients for visual inspection and does not measure bit depth or certify hardware.

Why could I see banding in a browser?

It can come from the source, browser or graphics pipeline, display processing or compression. The pattern alone cannot isolate the cause.
