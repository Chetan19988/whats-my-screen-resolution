How to Test Microphone and Audio

Input permission and output playback are controlled independently.

STEP 01

Start microphone input

Choose Start Microphone and allow access in the browser prompt. Speak and watch the meter. Stop the microphone when the check is complete.

STEP 02

Record only if needed

Start an optional recording, speak briefly, then stop it to enable playback. The recording stays in this page unless you choose Download Recording.

STEP 03

Check output channels

Set a low volume and press Play Left, Play Right or Play Center Tone. Use headphones to distinguish channels, then press Stop Tone if needed.

i

Privacy, Permissions & Measurement Notes

Browser audio tools provide simple functional checks, not studio measurements.

Permission and secure context

Microphone access is requested only when you press Start Microphone. Modern browsers generally require HTTPS or localhost. The browser permission prompt and site settings control access.

Local processing

The meter uses browser audio analysis, and optional recording is created with the browser's recording API. No audio is transmitted by this page. A saved recording is downloaded to your device only when requested.

Not calibrated audio equipment

The meter does not measure calibrated decibels, noise floor, frequency response or microphone quality. Test tones verify that browser playback is possible, but cannot certify speakers, headphones, channel balance or hearing safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the microphone test upload or save audio?

No. Audio is processed in the browser. Recording is optional and remains in the page session unless you explicitly download it.

When is microphone permission requested?

Only after you press Start Microphone. Stop Microphone ends and releases the active audio track.

Is the level meter a decibel measurement?

No. It shows relative signal level received by the browser, not calibrated loudness, frequency response or noise floor.

Why can I not hear a speaker test tone?

Check system volume, output device, mute state and browser audio permissions. Begin at a low volume; some browsers require a user click before playback.

Does the left/right test verify my hardware?

It requests panned tones through browser audio output. System routing, mono output and spatial audio effects may change what you hear, so it is a basic listening check only.

Why is my microphone missing?

Grant permission after pressing Start Microphone, connect or enable the device, and check browser site settings. Device labels may be hidden until permission is allowed.
