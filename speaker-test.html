How the Speaker Test Works

When you press Play Tone, the browser creates a brief sine-wave tone using the Web Audio API and sends it to the currently selected system output. No sound plays automatically and no audio is uploaded.

If you hear nothing, check system volume, mute state, output-device selection, browser audio settings and any connected speaker power.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this measure speaker quality?

No. It checks whether the browser can play a tone through the current output route. It does not measure frequency response, loudness or distortion.

Why start at low volume?

Test tones are simple signals and can sound loud or sharp. Begin at a low setting and increase carefully if needed.

Does audio play automatically?

No. Playback begins only after you press Play Tone, and you can stop it immediately.

Can I test headphones here?

Yes. Select your headphones as the system output before playing the tone. For separate channels, use the Left Right Audio Test.
