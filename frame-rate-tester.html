Understanding Motion Clarity & Judder

Moving images on a digital display are discrete snapshots presented sequentially. When the frame rate fails to match your display refresh rate or drops down to low thresholds, individual frames linger on the retina longer, creating visible stepping artifacts.

Display Native (Uncapped)

Updates on every single vertical refresh cycle supported by your graphics hardware, maximizing eye-tracking smoothness and minimizing perceived blur.

60 FPS Standard

The baseline standard for web browsing and modern user interfaces. Motion appears fluid, though slight trailing is visible at higher speeds.

30 FPS Video Rate

Common in console gaming and video playback. You will observe noticeable jitter, edge doubling, and reduced responsiveness compared to 60 FPS.

15 FPS Low-End

Substantial frame latency causing pronounced stutter. The dot noticeably jumps rather than gliding across the canvas track.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the relationship between FPS and Hz?

Hz (Hertz) is the physical capability of your screen panel—how many times per second the physical hardware redraws the image. FPS (Frames Per Second) is how many new frames your GPU and browser actively generate. To experience true 120Hz or 144Hz smoothness, your browser must render at matching frame rates. Not sure what pixel grid you are pushing? Check your screen resolution first, then come back here to measure how smoothly it renders.

Why does my screen appear locked at 60 FPS on a 120Hz/144Hz monitor?

Browsers automatically lock to your operating system's reported display frequency. If you possess a high-refresh display: verify your OS Display Advanced Settings are actively configured to 120Hz+, confirm browser hardware acceleration is turned on, and ensure your laptop is not in battery saver mode.

What causes frame drops and 1% Low stutters?

Background tasks, CPU throttling, complex DOM repaints, or garbage collection spikes in your browser can cause individual frames to take longer than the 16.6ms budget. The "1% Low" metric isolates these momentary hitches.

Does this tool transmit any system telemetry?

No. All calculations, timing measurements, and canvas renders execute entirely client-side using JavaScript's native requestAnimationFrame and high-resolution timing APIs.
