About the FPS Estimate

Track setting

The page shows the frame-rate value reported by the active browser media track when available.

Observed updates

The test samples video frame callbacks when supported, or compares rendered frames as a fallback. It reports what the page observed during that sample.

Private by design

The preview and frame sampling happen locally. This page does not upload camera video.

How to Test Camera Frame Rate

STEP 01

Start the camera

Press Start Camera and grant browser permission. Close other apps that may be using the camera if it will not open.

STEP 02

Measure updates

Keep the preview active and press Measure 5 Seconds. A moving scene and steady lighting can help the browser observe changed frames.

STEP 03

Compare cautiously

Compare the observation with the track-reported setting and device specifications. Different values do not by themselves indicate a fault.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the observed FPS the camera's true frame rate?

No. This is a browser-observed estimate. Browser scheduling, camera mode, lighting, processing load and display refresh can affect it.

When is camera permission requested?

Only after Start Camera is pressed. Stop releases the camera track.

Why is the observed value low?

The selected stream mode, browser, low light, static scenery, system load or display refresh may reduce visible updates.
